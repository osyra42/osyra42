## ☕ About Me

Over a decade of building across disciplines — AI-powered Discord bots with VTuber avatars, a custom Markdown parser that runs my whole site, a pixel dungeon crawler, cross-platform launcher tooling, browser utilities, educational worksheet generators, 3D-printed designs, and an ongoing sci-fi noir series with its own nested wiki. I also run a three-server Minecraft community — vanilla, modded, and a private group — with 14+ years of hosting behind it.

Everything I make lives under one roof at **[coffeebyte.dev](https://coffeebyte.dev)**.
